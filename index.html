about project

kira is an independent chess engine built from the ground up, one move at a time.

it combines its own move generation and search with a neural evaluation trained on games kira played against itself. the result is an engine shaped by its own experience.

kira is open source and speaks UCI, so you can explore how it works or bring it into your chess GUI.
